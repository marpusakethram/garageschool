Coming soon.

The GarageSchool Residency, for founders and investors.

We are building a residency where people who start companies and people who back them learn side by side. More details soon.

Get notifiedWhat we are planning

Open to founders and investors

One residency, two kinds of members. The format is still being designed. Here is who we are designing it for.

For founders

For people about to start a company, or in its first months.

  • Test ideas with people who will tell you the truth
  • Find cofounders and early collaborators
  • Learn how investors judge a company before you raise
  • Practice your pitch with people who invest for a living

For investors

For aspiring and early-career investors, scouts, and angels.

  • Meet founders before they raise, not after
  • Practice sourcing, diligence, and memos on real companies
  • Build judgment with a small group of peers
  • Learn from operators and experienced investors

Hear about it first

Leave your details and we will email you when dates, format, and how to apply are ready. No spam, and we never share your details.
