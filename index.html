Sourcing deals

Most of an investor's week is spent finding companies before others do. Learn how deal flow works, how to screen a pitch in ten minutes, and why you should keep a record of every pass.

Read the lesson

Judging founders

At the earliest stages, the team is most of the bet. Learn which traits predict success, how to run a founder meeting, and how to get references that tell you something real.

Read the lesson

Reading traction

Revenue growth, retention, gross margin, and burn tell you whether a product is working. Learn which numbers matter at each stage and which ones are vanity.

Read the lesson

Valuation and terms

Price changes your return as much as the company does. Build cap tables, read term sheets line by line, and see how dilution works round by round.

Try the workshops

Portfolio management

One or two companies drive most of a fund's result. Learn how investors run boards, decide on follow-ons, and support the companies that are breaking out.

See the course

Fund math

Fees, carry, reserves, and the J-curve decide what LPs actually receive. Run a fund in the partner tests and read the LP report at the end.

Try a partner test
