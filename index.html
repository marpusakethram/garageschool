    Questions

    Questions people ask

    What is GarageSchool?

    GarageSchool is a free school for venture capital. You learn the job by making investment decisions in simulated funds, then reading what happened to every company, alongside courses for investors and founders.

    How does a GarageSchool investing test work?

    Each test gives you a fund and 50 fictional startups. Analysts advance the 10 best deals, run due diligence and write an IC memo. Associates choose 10 companies to back. Partners make 10 to 18 first checks over three years and decide on follow-ons. A debrief then shows how every company turned out.

    Who is GarageSchool for?

    People who want to work in venture capital, such as aspiring analysts and associates, scouts and angels, and founders who want to understand how investors decide.

    Do I need a finance background?

    No. The courses start from the basics, there is a searchable glossary, and the first tests use small funds and early-stage deals.

    Are the companies and returns real?

    No. Every company and outcome is fictional and generated by the simulation. Nothing on GarageSchool is investment advice.

    How long does a test take?

    Plan on about an hour for a full test, and stop whenever you like because progress is saved. The example test has six startups and takes about five minutes with no account.

    How much does GarageSchool cost?

    The tests and courses are free. Create an account to save your progress.

    A residency for founders and investors that is coming soon. Dates, format and cost have not been announced yet. You can join the list on the residency page to hear first.
