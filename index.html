How a test works

Every test follows the same four steps, whichever track you choose.

  1. Read the fund brief

    The fund size, its thesis, how many companies you can back, and the size of each check.

  2. Read 50 deals

    Team, market, customers, traction, competition, risks, and terms. No scores to lean on.

  3. Make the call

    Back it or let it go. Decisions are final, and diligence calls are limited.

  4. Read the debrief

    What happened to all 50, how your picks compare with random ones, and which lesson to read next.
